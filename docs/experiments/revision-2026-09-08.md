# Experiment corrections and validation — 8 September 2026

## Weighted fields and derivative policy

Write `X = Omega chi`, `Xbar = Omega chibar`, `W = Omega omega`,
`Wbar = Omega omegabar`, `A = tr(X)`, and `P4 = partial_v phi`. The outgoing
weighted Raychaudhuri equation is

```text
partial_v A + 4 W A + A^2/2 + |hat(X)|^2 + P4^2 = 0.
```

The coefficient 4 includes the product rule for weighting the expansion by
`Omega`. The incoming equation has the corresponding coefficient and shift
advection. The incoming lapse at an integer iterate includes the correction
caused by the shift changing after the half iterate. Regression tests check
nonconstant-lapse identities and detect a deliberately incorrect coefficient.

The rough-data audits use first null derivatives of the stored weighted
connections. They do not differentiate a metric-derived connection in a null
direction a second time. In particular, `Ric44` is checked through the trace
Raychaudhuri equation, without differentiating outgoing shear in `v` or
evaluating the extreme curvature component `R4A4B`. Intrinsic sphere curvature
and the MOTS graph Hessian still require smooth angular derivatives. Separate
first-derivative checks cover metric/connection, both lapse equations,
shift/torsion, and scalar consistency. These residuals are numerical
diagnostics, not bounds on the metric error.

Independent angular transfer now uses the scalar, tangent-vector, and
symmetric-tensor harmonic spaces appropriate to each field. The former scalar
fit of every ambient component discarded geometric tensor modes and product
modes in the weighted forms. The corrected transfer preserves the resolvable
typed work band and uses a denser independent sphere grid. An analytic
tensor-harmonic regression demonstrates the failure of the old transfer and
the accuracy of the corrected one.

Experiment 7 compares every refinement on the same coordinate grid and
protected mask. Its audit partition includes every source-element boundary,
and its degrees are the largest source degrees plus three. Changing samples
near endpoint and interface layers therefore cannot create the reported
refinement trend. Construction and independent residuals have different norms
and masks; their ordering is recorded descriptively. Decreasing coordinate
and angular residuals, decreasing consistency defects, and small Picard
updates remain acceptance requirements.

Residual plots exclude outer endpoint placeholders before interpolation.
Experiment 5 retains its six-term weighted Ricci sum and reports `Ric44`
separately. Experiments 4, 7, and 8 use the positive null-component norm stated
in the article.

## Configuration and reproducibility

Experiment mode is explicit in TOML and no longer depends on the filename.
Each run writes a parameter contract listing adjustable controls and fixed
protocol descriptors. Unsupported changes fail before evolution. Summaries
record the actual refinement and audit grids. Resolved configurations can be
loaded again, with their derived dimensions checked.

Experiment 7 uses analytic angular profiles with grid-independent
normalization and explicit solver callbacks. Experiment 5 smoke runs use the
declared amplitude 0.1 and tolerance `1e-8`; standard runs explicitly use
amplitude 1.0. The strong-pulse wrapper disables unrequested sweep checkpoints.
Resume validates and reuses a completed immutable run; it does not claim to
continue an interrupted computation.

Result comparisons reject missing fields, type or shape mismatches, empty
comparisons, and nonfinite values. Inventories cover every case and record
array schemas and hashes. Installed-package provenance identifies the code
actually imported.

The large changes in residuals are explained by changed diagnostics on
unchanged states. For Experiment 4, the old protected value `2.453e-3` becomes
`2.617e-5`; the denser independent angular check confirms the latter. For
Experiment 5, the old six-term maximum `5.0216e-3` becomes `3.4319e-3` under
the first-order audit. The initial Experiment 7 audit failed its refinement
gate and correctly withheld the stress matrix. The tensor-transfer defect and
inconsistent refinement masks were then corrected and the evolution repeated.
The initial Experiment 4 export also rejected a one-ulp coordinate mismatch;
replaying the canonical logarithmic coordinate grid fixed that issue. Neither
failure was accepted as a completed publication run.

The two expected Experiment 7 rejections are `stress-axis-1-value-2.0` and
`stress-joint-2.0`: their incoming scalar-constraint radicands are negative
(`-0.0726794` and `-0.0729097`). These are inadmissible characteristic data.
The coordinate construction-residual maxima are not strictly decreasing,
although the independent residuals and native consistency envelopes decrease.
Angular consistency decreases only slightly at the finer levels, indicating
the remaining coordinate discretization floor. No claim of convergence for
every diagnostic or for the unprotected endpoint layer is made.

At the finest Experiment 7 coordinate level, common-grid consistency maxima
are `C3=6.4105e-5`, `C4=2.0378e-5`, scalar `P3=4.3816e-5`, and scalar
`P4=8.2611e-6`. For Experiment 4, protected metric/connection defects are
`C3=1.7433e-9` and `C4=2.7653e-7`. For Experiment 5, native protected defects
are `C3=3.0974e-4`, `C4=1.8458e-5`, outgoing lapse `4.1358e-8`, incoming
lapse `4.2914e-5`, and shift/torsion `4.7309e-5`. These separate checks expose
the finite discretization error left after the Picard updates settle.

In Experiment 8, the trapped coordinate section at
`(u,v)=(-0.4696271025,0.04)` has expansion suprema `-0.0802152` and
`-4.28011`; the angular control gives `-0.0802016` and `-4.27999`.
The coordinate-refined horizon graph changes by at most `1.5005e-7`, with
relative area change `6.5423e-7`. The angular control changes the mean graph
by `7.7987e-10` and the area relatively by `3.2945e-9`. The corrected anchor
curvature residual `0.0191450` changes relatively by only `6.9e-10` under
an additional 400-point, degree-11 angular transfer.

MOTS acceptance checks nonlinear solver success, finite positive area and
patch margin, outgoing-residual tolerance, negative incoming expansion, and
sign brackets on displaced graphs. Rejected attempts are retained. Degree
three is a diagnostic comparison, and outermostness among arbitrary angular
graphs is not claimed.
